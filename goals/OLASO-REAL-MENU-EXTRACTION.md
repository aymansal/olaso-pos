# Olaso Real Menu Extraction

## Purpose

This document is a factual transcription of the seven owner-supplied menu
images received on 2 September 2026. It is the durable source for discussing
how the real café menu should be configured in the existing product, size,
choice, extra, recipe, and category tools.

It is not authorization to import or change live menu data. Do not invent
recipes, ingredient quantities, product availability, or corrected spellings.

## Source and interpretation rules

- Prices are in MAD.
- Source spellings are preserved when the image is readable, even when a word
  may be misspelled.
- `Secret Menu` and `Best Seller` are presentation badges, not options.
- Marketing notes such as `UJI Origin`, `Samidori Cultivars`, `Stone-milled`,
  `Homemade daily`, and `Fresh locally sourced ingredients` are not options.
- House-made syrup and V60 origin lists do not show individual prices. They
  must not be made paid choices without owner confirmation.
- The brioche descriptions identify ingredients, but the images do not provide
  stock quantities. Do not infer recipe quantities from them.
- The seven images contain 92 priced menu listings when
  `Vanille Madagascar / Yogurt` is one listing and V60 is one product with two
  variants. Splitting either ambiguous listing would increase that count.

## Suggested mapping to existing app tools

This is a proposed configuration shape, not an instruction to import data:

- Create the seven source sections as categories after the owner confirms their
  names and order.
- Keep each named and priced menu listing as a product. Do not duplicate a
  flavor as a product choice when the menu already prices it as a separate
  product.
- Use product sizes for Standard/Plus and for V60 Hot/Cold. Each category keeps
  its own shown increment: Matcha +20, Lemonades +15, Coffee +15.
- Use a single-choice milk group where milk applies. Whole would normally be
  the default. The likely price mapping is Whole +0, Lactose Free +5, and
  Oat/Coconut/Almond +10 through the menu’s `Vegetal Milk` price, but the owner
  must confirm that mapping before configuration.
- Use an optional single-choice coffee syrup group with the seven listed
  flavors and the shown +5 MAD syrup charge, if each syrup has the same price.
- Use the named extras as product-owned choices with their displayed price.
  Whether multiple extras can be selected together must be confirmed where the
  source does not say.
- Keep Secret Menu and Best Seller as source notes unless a later approved app
  feature represents badges.
- Build recipes only from the café’s real ingredient quantities. The images
  alone are not enough to deduct stock correctly.
